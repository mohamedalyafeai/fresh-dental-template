# Bright Smile Design

The "Modern & Professional" (Best for General Dentistry)
This creates a clean, trustworthy look suitable for a standard family clinic.

Prompt: "Build a professional and clean website for a dental clinic called [Insert Clinic Name]. The design should be modern, minimalist, and use a color palette of medical blue, white, and soft grey to convey trust and hygiene.

Required Sections:

Hero Section: High-quality image of a smiling patient with a clear 'Book Appointment' button.

About Us: A section introducing the dentists and the team.

Services: Grid layout highlighting General Dentistry, Teeth Whitening, Root Canals, and Emergency Care.

Testimonials: A slider showing patient reviews.

Footer: Location map, phone number, and opening hours.

Make the layout mobile-responsive and ensure the typography is easy to read."
